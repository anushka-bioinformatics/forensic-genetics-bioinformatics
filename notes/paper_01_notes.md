# Paper 1 name
##  Implementation of NGS and SNP microarrays in routine forensic practice: opportunities and barriers

## What I learned from the paper?
### STRs
-STRs are short tandem repeats.
- Highly polymorphic.
- Useful for DNA profiling.
- most abundant tandem sequence in human genome.
### SNPs
- SNPs are single nucleotide variations.
- Usually biallelic.
- Useful when DNA is degraded.
- Non binary SNPs can be tri/tetra allelic.

## Questions
1. How many STR loci are used in forensic databases?
2. How many SNPs are needed to match STR discrimination power?

Understanding SNPs and STRs. Additional knowledge on NGS, CE and CE STR typing.

## Recap
